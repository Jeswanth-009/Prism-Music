// 07 — Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

01 Why is Prism Music distributed via APK instead of the Play Store?

Prism Music streams directly from public music catalogs and provides built-in offline downloads without account lock-in. Sideloading allows the app to remain completely free, ad-free, and independent of proprietary store restrictions.

02 Is it truly free? Is there any subscription or catch?

It is 100% free and open source under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertisements, or tracking SDKs.

03 How does the infinite radio recommendation work?

When you search for and tap any song, Prism Music starts playback of that seed song and immediately queries the algorithmic recommendation feed, automatically queueing new tracks that match its style.

04 Where are my downloads and playlists saved?

All downloaded songs, playlists, and listening statistics are stored locally on your device in app-private storage. You can also export portable JSON backups from the Settings menu at any time.

05 Can I import my playlists from Spotify or YouTube?

Yes. Simply copy your public Spotify or YouTube playlist URL, navigate to Library → Import, and Prism Music will automatically resolve the tracks and save them to your device with automated exponential backoff and 429 rate-limit protection.

06 How does the dual-tier lyrics engine work offline?

Prism Music queries LRCLIB for line-synced lyrics first, falling back smoothly to a secondary provider if not found. Once loaded, lyrics are cached locally on your device so you can sing along without an active network connection.

07 Why is download concurrency capped at 3 tracks?

Prism Music uses an intelligent worker pool that caps concurrent active downloads at 3 songs while queueing any remaining requests. This prevents mobile network saturation, avoids socket exhaustion, and guarantees smooth background audio playback while downloading.
